# 📊 Playbook – Thursday, August 21 (Focus Europe & US)

**Local time: Europe/Paris (CEST)**  
**Key data windows: 09:15–10:30 (EU PMI) / 14:30–16:00 (US releases)**

---

## 🎯 Session Objective
Leverage **growth/employment surprises** between the **Eurozone** (France/Germany/Eurozone PMI) and the **United States** (Jobless Claims, Philly Fed, US PMI).

### Trading universe:
- **Core**: `EUR/JPY`, `EUR/USD`
- **Alternative (if EUR excluded)**: `AUD/USD`
- **Secondary**: `EUR/GBP` *(not prioritized)*

---

## 🔁 Golden Rules
- ✅ Only **buy EUR if EU data is strong**.  
- ❌ **No EUR trades if EU data is negative** (lack of macro consistency).  
- 📉 **Short EUR/USD only if EU neutral/weak AND US strong**.  
- 🔄 **If both EU & US disappoint → `AUD/USD long`** (USD sold, EUR not buyable).  
- ⚠️ **`EUR/GBP` = not prioritized** (only on strong UK surprise).  

---

## ⏱️ Execution Timeline

### 1) Europe Block (09:15–10:00) — FR/DE/EZ PMI
- **Positive surprise (>= expectations, near/above 50)**  
  🔹 **Preferred trade: `EUR/JPY long`** (risk-on momentum).  

- **Negative surprise (generalized <50)**  
  ❌ **No EUR trades**.  

- **Mixed (France beats, Germany misses, or vice versa)**  
  ❌ **No EUR trades**.  

---

### 2) UK Block (10:30) — Services PMI *(not prioritized)*
- **> 52.5** → possible **`EUR/GBP short`**.  
- **< 51.0** → possible **`EUR/GBP long`**.  
⚠️ Not a core trade (isolated, no global macro follow-through).  

---

### 3) US Block (14:30–16:00) — Claims, Philly Fed, US PMI

#### Case A: US disappoint  
- **If EU strong** → **`EUR/USD long`** (double flow: pro-EUR / anti-USD).  
- **If EU weak** → ❌ **No EUR trades** → **`AUD/USD long`** preferred.  

#### Case B: US strong (Claims <220K, Philly >10, Services PMI >55)  
- **If EU neutral/weak** → **`EUR/USD short`** (USD strength vs EUR fragile).  
- **If EU strong** → ❌ **No EUR/USD trade** (opposing forces, no momentum).  

---

## ✅ Final Summary

- **EU strong**
  - Morning → `EUR/JPY long`.
  - Afternoon (US disappoints) → `EUR/USD long`.
  - Afternoon (US strong) → ❌ No EUR/USD trade.

- **EU neutral**
  - Morning → ❌ No trade.
  - Afternoon (US disappoints) → `EUR/USD long`.
  - Afternoon (US strong) → `EUR/USD short`.

- **EU weak**
  - ❌ No EUR trades (morning or afternoon).
  - Afternoon (US disappoints) → `AUD/USD long`.
  - Afternoon (US strong) → USD strengthens but not via EUR → possible `USD/JPY long` or `AUD/USD short`.
