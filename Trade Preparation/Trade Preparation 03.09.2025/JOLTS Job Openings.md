  - Forecast: 7.44M  
  - Previous: 7.39M

## 🔍 Why It Matters  

- **JOLTS**: key indicator of US labor market tightness → directly impacts Fed rate cut expectations.  
- **Factory Orders**: a gauge of industrial demand and business investment.  
- Current context: the market is already pricing a **Fed rate cut in September**. Weak data would reinforce this narrative → USD bearish.  

---

## 🔄 Market Reaction Filter: US 2Y Yields & DXY  

### 📉 US 2-Year Treasury Yield (T-Notes 2Y)  
- **Weak JOLTS / Factory Orders drop** → yields fall → USD bearish.  
- **Strong JOLTS / Resilient Orders** → yields rise → USD bullish.  

Useful trackers:  
- [FRED — DGS2](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS2)  
- [Investing.com — US 2Y Yield](https://www.investing.com/rates-bonds/u.s.-2-year-bond-yield)  

### 💵 DXY (US Dollar Index)  
- **If bearish USD confirmed** → DXY falls.  
- **If bullish USD confirmed** → DXY rises.  

⚖️ Both (2Y yields & DXY) must confirm before executing a trade.  

---

## 🎯 Scenarios  

### Scenario A — US Weakness (Base Case)  
- **JOLTS < 7.3M** or **Factory Orders ≤ -5.5%**  
- Signs of labor market cooling + demand weakness  
- **USD bearish**  
- **Action**:  
  - If **AUD GDP ≥ 0.5%** + risk-on → **Go long AUD/USD**  
  - If **AUD GDP ≤ 0.2%** or risk-off → **Go long EUR/USD**  

---

### Scenario B — US Strength (Invalidation)  
- **JOLTS > 7.5M** or **Factory Orders > -3%**  
- Market reprices a less dovish Fed stance → USD bullish  
- **Action**:  
  - ❌ **No trade** (stay in cash, maintain discipline)  

---

### Scenario C — Mixed / Neutral  
- Data broadly in line (JOLTS ~7.4M, Orders between -3% and -5%)  
- Market reaction choppy, no clear flows  
- **Action**:  
  - ❌ **No trade**

| Principle                           | Action                                         |

- **Main plan**: play USD weakness if US data disappoints.
