# 📊 Detailed Hypotheses — EUR/USD (ECB + US CPI Day)

## 🟢 Scenario A — EUR/USD BULLISH (base case)
### Conditions
- **ECB (14:15 CET)**:
  - Rates unchanged (2.00% / 2.15%) but *statement turns hawkish*.
  - Inflation slightly above expectations (2.1% vs 2.0%).
  - Lagarde (14:45 & 16:15) reinforces the hawkish bias: “higher for longer”, vigilance on inflation.
- **US CPI (14:30 CET)**:
  - Downside surprise: YoY ≤ 2.8% (vs exp. 2.9%), or MoM ≤ 0.2% (vs exp. 0.3%), or Core ≤ 3.0% (vs exp. 3.1%).

### Interpretation
- Hawkish ECB + higher-than-expected Eurozone inflation = EUR supported.
- Softer CPI = dovish Fed repricing → USD under pressure.

---

## 🔴 Scenario B — EUR/USD BEARISH (secondary risk)
### Conditions
- **US CPI (14:30 CET)**:
  - Upside surprise: YoY ≥ 3.0%, or MoM ≥ 0.4%, or Core ≥ 3.2%.
- **ECB (14:15–14:45 CET)**:
  - Even if the statement is hawkish, the CPI surprise dominates short-term flows.

### Interpretation
- Stronger US inflation = Fed repriced hawkish.
- Market shifts flows back into USD strength.

---

## ⚪ Scenario C — EUR/USD NEUTRAL (range-bound)
### Conditions
- **US CPI (14:30 CET)**:
  - In line with consensus: YoY = 2.9%, MoM = 0.3%, Core = 3.1%.
- **ECB (14:15–14:45 CET)**:
  - Statement hawkish but without surprise.
  - Lagarde confirms vigilance but no new aggressive signal.

### Interpretation
- Both central banks remain in known stances → no major imbalance.
- EUR/USD trades sideways within range until a new catalyst emerges.
