### Market Idea — USD/JPY: High Probability of Downside Correction 

**Single Hypothesis**  
USD/JPY continues its upward trend despite a **tightening spread** between US 10-year yields and Japanese 10-year JGBs. This **decoupling** of bond signals and the FX market, coupled with a **bearish momentum divergence** (RSI falling while price remains elevated), suggests the dollar’s strength may lack support. I expect FX will eventually **catch down** to the bond logic via a downside correction in USD/JPY.

---

**Drivers / Rationale**  
- The US10Y – JGB10Y spread shows compression, reducing the yield differential in favor of the dollar.  
- RSI and other momentum indicators are signaling waning strength in the upward move.  
- Japan’s internal context is uncertain: the new Prime Minister and anticipated monetary/fiscal shifts may cap further yen weakness.  
- Upcoming US releases (Powell’s speech, Unemployment Claims) could trigger or accelerate the reversal if they are dovish.

---

**Trade Scenario (Operational Plan)**  
- Wait for a **bearish breakout** of a trendline or structural support on USD/JPY.  
- Seek confirmation via volume / momentum / continued narrowing of the yield spread.  
- Place stop just above the recent resistance.  
- Target an earlier support zone (to be defined per timeframe: e.g. ~150.500 or ~149.500).

---

**Invalidation Conditions**  
- USD/JPY convincingly breaks resistance to the upside.  
- The yield spread **widens again in favor of the dollar** (US10Y rises or JGB10Y falls).  
- US data or Powell’s speech remain hawkish, reinforcing dollar strength.
