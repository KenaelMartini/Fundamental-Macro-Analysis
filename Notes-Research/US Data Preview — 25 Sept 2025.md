---

## 2. Final GDP q/q
- **Above 3.3%** → USD stronger (growth > expected, Fed less dovish). → **Bearish EUR/USD**  
- **At 3.3%** → Neutral, priced in.  
- **Below 3.3%** → USD weaker (growth < expected, risk of downgrades). → **Bullish EUR/USD**  

⚠️ Final release = usually low impact unless deviation ≥0.2pp.  

---

## 3. Unemployment Claims
- **Above 233k (higher claims)** → USD weaker, **Bullish EUR/USD**  
- **Below 233k (lower claims)** → USD stronger, **Bearish EUR/USD**  
- **At 233k** → Neutral, priced in.  

⚠️ More market-sensitive than Final GDP (direct labor market signal).  

---

## 4. Combined Scenarios
- **Strong GDP + Low Claims** → USD double support → **EUR/USD down**  
- **Weak GDP + High Claims** → USD double weakness → **EUR/USD up**  
- **Mixed data** → choppy price action, short-lived moves.  

---

## 5. Desk Bias
- **Primary driver = Claims > GDP**  
- **EUR/USD** bias = long if labor data disappoints, short if labor data surprises positively.  
- Refrain from trading if numbers land exactly at consensus.
